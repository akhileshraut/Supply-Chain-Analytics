# 🚚 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed for the **ZoomCharts 4U Report Challenge – May 2026**, focused on analyzing end-to-end food & beverage supply chain operations.

The report enables supply chain managers and executives to monitor operational performance, identify inefficiencies, and optimize fulfillment processes using interactive ZoomCharts visuals.

---

# 📌 Challenge Overview

This challenge is based on a real-world food & beverage supply chain dataset covering:

* Orders & Deliveries
* Warehouses & Suppliers
* Inventory & Stockouts
* Returns & Waste
* Delivery Performance (OTIF)
* Financial Metrics (Revenue, Profit, COGS)

The goal was to build an executive-friendly analytical report that connects operational performance with financial outcomes.

---

# 🎯 Report Objectives

The dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:

* How reliable is the delivery process?
* Which suppliers or warehouses are underperforming?
* Where are stockouts, returns, or quality issues occurring?
* How do operational issues impact profitability?
* Which products or channels require optimization?

# 📄 Report Structure

## Page 1 — Supply Chain Overview

Executive-level monitoring page focused on:

* Revenue & Profitability
* Fulfillment Volume
* Reverse Logistics
* Inventory Risk
* KPI Trends & Operational Overview

### KPIs Included

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Order Quantity
* Return Quantity
* Stockout %

### Features

* Dynamic KPI Selector using Field Parameters
* Interactive ZoomCharts Drill Down visuals
* Dynamic KPI narratives
* PM vs Current Period comparisons
* Executive storytelling layout

---

## Page 2 — Operational Intelligence
